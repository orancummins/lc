Dynamic Mechanisms (Leaving Cert D.C.G.) – Simple Explanation
In Design and Communication Graphics, a dynamic mechanism is a machine or system that uses moving parts to change one type of motion into another.
In simple terms:
A dynamic mechanism is something that moves and makes something else move in a controlled way.
What “dynamic” means
Dynamic = moving
So dynamic mechanisms are machines with motion, not static drawings.
They are used to:
transmit motion
change speed
change direction
change type of motion
The main idea
A dynamic mechanism usually has:
an input motion (what you do to it)
a mechanism (the moving parts inside)
an output motion (what it produces)
Easy examples
1. Bicycle gears
Your legs turn the pedals (input)
Gears transfer the motion
The wheel turns faster or slower (output)
👉 Changes speed and force
2. Car windscreen wipers
A motor spins in circles
Linkages change circular motion into back-and-forth motion
Wipers sweep across the screen
👉 Changes circular motion into oscillating motion
3. Door hinge
You push the door (linear motion)
It rotates around the hinge
👉 Changes linear motion into rotary motion
Types of motion you need to know
In exams, you often describe how mechanisms change motion:
Rotary motion → spinning (like wheels)
Linear motion → straight line (like a drawer sliding)
Reciprocating motion → back and forth in a straight line (like a piston)
Oscillating motion → back and forth in an arc (like a pendulum)
Common exam mechanisms
You should recognise these:
Crank and slider mechanism (engine pistons)
Cam and follower (turning motion into lifting motion)
Gear systems (speed changes)
Linkages (changing direction of motion)
Cam and follower (very important)
A cam is a shaped rotating piece.
A follower moves up and down when the cam turns.
👉 Used in:
car engines
automatic machines
toys
Why they are important in DCG exams
You are often asked to:
describe motion changes
draw labelled diagrams
explain input/output motion
identify mechanisms in real-life objects
Simple exam sentence you can use
“A dynamic mechanism is a system of moving parts that transmits and transforms motion from an input motion to an output motion.”
Quick memory trick
Think:
“Move in → mechanism → different move out”
