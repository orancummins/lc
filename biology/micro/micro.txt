1️⃣ Bacterial Cell Structure (VERY COMMON)
6
Must label:
Cell wall
Cell membrane
Cytoplasm
Nucleoid (DNA loop)
Ribosomes
Flagellum (movement)
Capsule (protection)

👉 Exam focus: no nucleus (prokaryote)

2️⃣ Virus Structure (VERY FREQUENT)
8
Must label:
Capsid (protein coat)
Genetic material (DNA/RNA)
Envelope (sometimes)
Spikes (attachment proteins)

👉 Key idea: not a living cell

3️⃣ Yeast Cell (FUNGUS – VERY COMMON)
6
Must label:
Cell wall
Nucleus
Cytoplasm
Vacuole
Budding cell (important process)

👉 Key process: budding reproduction

4️⃣ Microscopic Plant vs Animal Cell (MICROBIOLOGY FOUNDATION)
6
Plant cell labels:
Cell wall
Chloroplasts
Large vacuole
Animal cell labels:
No cell wall
No chloroplasts
Small vacuoles

5️⃣ Bacterial Reproduction (Binary Fission)
4
Steps to know:
DNA replicates
Cell elongates
Cell membrane pinches in
Two identical cells form

👉 Key word: asexual reproduction

🧠 WHAT EXAMINERS WANT (VERY IMPORTANT)

For any microbiology diagram:

✔ neat outline
✔ correct labels
✔ correct scale
✔ arrows for processes (if needed)
✔ clear differences (virus vs cell is a favourite question)

📌 MOST LIKELY EXAM QUESTIONS

You should be ready for:

Draw and label a bacterial cell
Describe the structure of a virus
Show binary fission in bacteria
Compare plant and animal cells
Explain why viruses are not living organisms
