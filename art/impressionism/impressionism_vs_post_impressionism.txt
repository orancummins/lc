🎨 IMPRESSIONISM vs POST-IMPRESSIONISM CHEAT SHEET
🌤️ IMPRESSIONISM (c. 1870–1890)
🧠 BIG IDEA

Impressionism = capturing a “moment in time”

👉 Not realistic detail
👉 Focus on light, colour, atmosphere

🎨 KEY FEATURES
Loose, visible brushstrokes
Bright, natural light
Outdoor painting (plein air)
Everyday scenes (no grand history paintings)
No black outlines
Shadows = coloured (not black/grey)
👨‍🎨 KEY ARTISTS
🎯 Claude Monet
Painted the same scene at different times
Famous for water lilies, light effects
🎯 Edgar Degas
Focus on dancers, movement
Indoor + unusual viewpoints
🎯 Pierre-Auguste Renoir
Social scenes, people enjoying life
Soft, warm colours
🖼️ EXAM KEY PHRASES
“capture the impression of light”
“momentary effects of atmosphere”
“painted outdoors directly from observation”
🌅 TYPICAL SUBJECTS
Landscapes
City streets
Leisure scenes
Water, light reflections
🌄 POST-IMPRESSIONISM (c. 1885–1910)
🧠 BIG IDEA

Post-Impressionism = reaction against Impressionism

👉 More structure
👉 More emotion
👉 More symbolic meaning

🎨 KEY FEATURES
Stronger shapes and structure
Bold outlines in some works
Distorted forms for expression
Bright but often unnatural colours
Focus on emotion, not realism
👨‍🎨 KEY ARTISTS
🎯 Vincent van Gogh
Emotional brushwork
Swirling movement
Intense colour use
🎯 Paul Cézanne
“Father of modern art”
Built forms using geometric shapes
Influenced Cubism
🎯 Paul Gauguin
Used symbolic colour
Exotic, simplified scenes
Lived in Tahiti
🖼️ EXAM KEY PHRASES
“art based on emotion rather than observation”
“simplified forms and structure”
“colour used expressively, not realistically”
🌋 TYPICAL SUBJECTS
Portraits
Emotional landscapes
Symbolic scenes
Everyday life but exaggerated
⚖️ IMPRESSIONISM vs POST-IMPRESSIONISM (IMPORTANT COMPARISON)
Impressionism 🌤️	Post-Impressionism 🌄
Light & atmosphere	Emotion & structure
Real-life observation	Personal interpretation
Loose brushwork	Stronger shapes/forms
Outdoor scenes	Symbolic + emotional work
Moment captured	Meaning expressed
🧠 EASY MEMORY TRICK

👉 Impressionism = “I see light”
👉 Post-Impressionism = “I feel it”

✏️ HOW TO GET FULL MARKS IN EXAMS

Always mention:

Artist name
Style features
Subject matter
What makes it different from earlier art
At least one specific example painting (Monet water lilies, Van Gogh Starry Night, etc.)
